1. Information We Collect

Make my Mate collects only the details you provide when creating a flatmate account: email address, name, age, occupation, target Indian city/locality, monthly budget, lifestyle preferences (sleep, cleaning, smoking, pets), flat listings, chat messages with your matches, and optional verification references (last 4 digits of Aadhaar, masked PAN, or work email).

2. How We Use & Protect Your Data

Your data is used strictly to calculate roommate compatibility, display profiles to signed-in users, support real-time match chats, and verify user identities. All data is stored securely on Supabase PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security (RLS). We never sell your personal information to brokers or advertisers.

3. How to Delete Your Account & Data

Option A (Instant, Inside App): Sign into Make my Mate → tap the Account icon in the top-right header → tap Delete My Account & All Data → Confirm Delete. This immediately deletes your profile, preferences, swipes, matches, chat messages, listings, and KYC records.

Option B (Web / Email Request): Email suyashbabar17@gmail.com from your registered email with the subject "Delete Make my Mate Account". All associated data will be permanently erased within 48 hours.
